The Full Continuous Message (All Names Connected):
Man is appointed mortal sorrow,
but the Blessed God shall come down teaching;
His death shall bring the despairing rest.
The Name is a stronghold beyond division;
the Friend, the Branch, after delay—
the Father of many.
Laughter for the supplanter becomes praise through breakthrough.
Enclosed yet exalted,
a willing people confront the serpent,
clothed in strength—
the Servant, the Gift, the Beloved.
Peace enlarges the people.
My Father is Yahweh the Healer;
Yahweh has judged and is exalted.
Yahweh is strength and upright;
He has grasped and strengthens,
causing sin to be forgotten.
The Faithful One—Yahweh supports and establishes.
Asked of God, the Seed in Babylon:
My Father is majesty; God raises up the Helper,
the Righteous One whom God establishes.
God is majesty; God has helped.
The Gift to the supplanter—
He will add:
Yahweh saves.
